About PKR

More Than Refrigeration. We Engineer Complete Cold Solutions.

Pak kooling & Refrigeration (Pvt.) Ltd. (PKR) is a Pakistan-based cold-storage, cold-chain and industrial refrigeration engineering company serving commercial and industrial requirements.

Our approach goes beyond equipment supply. We focus on complete temperature-controlled environments — from project planning and engineering to installation, integration and commissioning.
